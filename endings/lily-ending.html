"Ava, I did it. I revealed the truth about your job at the bakery."
You felt a rush of emotions, but you didn't know if you were sad or angry. "Lily... why?"

"I don't know, it just... happened. I didn't mean to hurt you at all. I'm really sorry, Ava."

You couldn't believe that Lily, your best friend, would do this to you. All you could feel now was anger. "You didn't mean to hurt me? You should know how much this would hurt me. Do you even remember that my parents are divorced—"

"Yes, I remember that. Okay honest? I was... peer pressured into telling. Like, a freaking mountain of pressure. So I told them about it. I'm so sorry, Ava."

Your anger was suddenly replaced by sadness. Part of you knew it was hard for Lily, but another part of you just couldn't accept her apology so the only thing you could think of saying is, "Goodbye, Lily."
