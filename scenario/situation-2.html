Situation

Middle school was pretty great. You had a lot of fun and made some friends, but one day, everything changed.

Not long after you got your job, people in your middle school started whispering about it. A group of middle schoolers snickered as they passed your lonely table at lunch, and you overheard them say:

"Ava has a job? So performative. Why would she even have a job in middle school? Like, is she poor or something?"

Shocked and embarrassed, you began to dread school every single day. It was already hard enough at home and work, but school too? The constant whispering, the snickering, the bullying...

Just when you thought you couldn't take it anymore, you found out that your classmates had been secretly talking about you having a job and being "poor." Although this was true, you hadn't told anyone about it except for 3 people...
