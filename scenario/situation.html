Situation (Ava's Past)

Your parents divorced when you were in grade 4. You remember the fight before everything happened: the screaming, the tears...

You decided to move in with your mother. Your father flew to another city without a word. Since then, your mother has been working 24/7, but even that wasn't enough to support both of you. Due to their financial struggles, you had picked up a job at a local bakery as soon as you could, which was in grade 7. On top of that, you had school work and chores at home: cooking, cleaning, and doing the laundry.

No one knew about this except for a few people you were close to at school.
