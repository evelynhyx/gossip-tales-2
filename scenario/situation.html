Situation (Ava's Past)

Ava finally entered middle school. She was so excited to meet new people and make new friends, but the mood quickly disappeared when her life turned upside down.

Ava's parents divorced at this time. She remembered the fight before everything happened: the screaming, the tears...

Ava decided to move in with her mother. Her father flew to another city. Since then, her mother has been working 24/7, but even that wasn't enough to support the two of them. Due to their financial struggles, Ava had picked up a job at a local bread shop. On top of that, she had school work and chores at home: cooking, cleaning, and doing the laundry.

Soon, in school, Ava's classmates began to notice that she had a job and they started to bully her for it.
